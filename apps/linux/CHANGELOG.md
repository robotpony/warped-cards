# Changelog: Crib RPG for Linux

The Linux build's changes, for players. Newest first. Style: CLAUDE.md › Commits.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-16

### Added

- The match turn: trace sets on the scoring grid by dragging or tapping, with cards in a combo lighting up as you trace and the help line saying what the set would score.
- Scored sets pop and clear, cards fall and new ones drop in from the stock. The steps number and track count up.
- Power jokers: the swapper, bomb, reach and burn, armed from the tray or the board.
- The pick counter, the turn clock and its timer, toasts, the left-over warning, info cards for the hand, crib, starter, power jokers and hand size, and the turn summary between turns.
- Control-N starts a new game and Control-R plays the same seed again.

### Changed

- The game is called Crib RPG, with its own icon.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-16

### Added

- The first Linux build. It opens a window with its build line and plays no game yet; the scenes come next.
