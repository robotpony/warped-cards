Maze tiles: three retro looks

Design exploration, 2026-11-14. Bruce asked for a retro look for the tiles and pieces, with modern considerations. Three directions, each drawing the same pieces. This page stands alone: maze-art.js and the maze walk stay as they are until a look is chosen.

Modern considerations, for every look

  • Clear at every size the game uses: 55 and 80 pt in the quarter view, 27 pt for the whole maze. Each look is shown at 55 and 27 pt.
  • Colour is never the only cue. Suits carry their glyph, bots differ by shape as well as colour, and doors carry their strength as a number.
  • Contrast aims at WCAG AA: 3:1 for walls and icons against their floor, 4.5:1 for numbers. Spades get a light plate or a light colour so they never sit black on dark.
  • Crisp on Retina screens: everything is vector, and pixel art snaps to an 11 × 11 grid per cell.
  • Glows, pulses and flicker are decoration only, and they stop under Reduce Motion. The retro font is only for short numbers and labels, never rules text.
