Crib Maze, mock v4 (Boggle-style, special cards, score breakdown), match rules , 2026-11-14. Every value is a starting guess.

How to play
  • Drag across up to 5 touching cards (diagonals count), like tracing a word in Boggle, and let go. Hand and crib cards touch only their neighbours, as grid cards do. The starter sits in the corner where your hand and the crib meet, tilted and tagged, and touches its neighbours too: the last hand card, the bottom crib card and the grid corner. Drag back to undo the last card.
  • Every set needs at least one grid card: your hand, crib and starter can't score on their own.
  • The set scores automatically, counted like a cribbage hand: every fifteen (2 each), pair (2), pair royal (6), double pair royal (12), run (1 per card; double runs count twice), flush of 4 or 5, and his nobs (1). 8-6-A is a fifteen, 2. 7-8-8 is two fifteens and a pair, 6. Every card must be part of at least one combo. If a card is left over, or nothing scores, nothing happens.
  • With taps instead of a drag: tap cards to select them, then tap the last one again to submit.
  • Scored grid cards clear, the cards above fall and the stock refills. Edge cards (gold) stay and power up: each level adds 1 to the pips tally on later scores, not steps. Pips buy jokers and special cards between rounds. The same set can't score twice.
  • Special cards wait in the tray at the bottom. Tap one to arm it: Swapper (⇄): then trace a straight line of 2 to 4 grid cards; the line is reversed. Bomb: then tap a grid card; it and its neighbours are cleared and refilled, scoring nothing.
  • Every score plays out part by part, like counting a hand aloud: the set's name and suit, each combo with its cards lit and a running total, edge power to pips, the power-up, then the total flying to the step counter. It starts slow and speeds up. Tap the phone to skip to the end. The timer pauses while it plays.
  • Each set's steps carry its most common suit, for suit gates in the maze. A tie carries no suit.
  • At the end of the turn, your best score wins one prize, a special card for your tray. Special cards are kept all game (this mock resets the tray on each New turn).
  • When the stock or card budget runs out, nothing refills: play out the cards that are left. Clear every grid card for the clear-board bonus. If no set can score, the mock says so; use a special card or end the turn.
  • The turn ends on the timer, End turn, or a cleared board.
Settings (apply on New turn)

The bot scores the best set it can find. When nothing scores it uses a swapper (on the swap that opens the best score), then a bomb, then stops.
