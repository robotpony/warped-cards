Match turn mock

Crib Maze, mock v9 (Boggle-style, special jokers, score breakdown, left-over cost, lit combos, joker reveals, begin and end effects, edge power multipliers), match rules , 2026-11-14. Every value is a starting guess.

The turn

  1. Drag across 2 to 5 touching cards (diagonals count) and let go. Or tap cards, then tap the last one again.
  2. The set scores at once, counted like a cribbage hand. Every set needs a grid card.
  3. The starter joins every set for free. If a combo uses it, it scores and powers up with the set; if not, it's ignored. Tracing it doesn't use up a pick.
  4. Scored grid cards clear and the stock refills. Your hand, crib and starter (gold) stay and power up: each level multiplies the later sets they're in.
  5. When the stock runs out, play out what's left.
  6. The turn ends on the timer, a cleared board, or End turn. Your best score wins a special joker.

Cards left over

A card in no combo is left over and costs 2 steps. Cards in a combo light up as you trace; a dimmer card would be left over.

Special and extra cards

Swapper (tray)Tap it, then trace a straight line of 2 to 4 grid cards to reverse it
Bomb (tray)Tap it, then tap a grid card: it and its neighbours clear
Jokers, title, blank (stock)Play pauses, something random happens, and the card becomes a prize: a new card (★, kept for the game), a special joker, steps or more
Special joker (on the board)Waits there until you tap it to use it. It can't be part of a set

Settings (apply on New turn)

The bot scores the best set it can find. When nothing scores it uses a swapper (on the swap that opens the best score), then a bomb, then stops.

Score breakdown (applies at once)

Begin and end effects

The turn begins with the grid dealing in column by column, then the hand, crib and starter. It ends with a celebration when you clear the board, or a quiet wrap-up when time, the stock or the sets run out. Tap to skip either. The timer starts after the deal.

Playtest runs Every finished turn is saved in this browser with its settings, hand, crib, starter, sets and score. "Classic count" is the hand plus crib as plain cribbage would score them.
