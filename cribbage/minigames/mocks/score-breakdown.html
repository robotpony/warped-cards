Crib Maze, the overlay that plays each score part by part. Separate from the match turn mock so it can grow as features are added. 2026-11-14. Every timing is a starting guess.

What plays, in order
  • The set lifts and the rest of the board dims.
  • The set's name (its biggest combo) shows as a banner, tagged SINGLE (one combo) or MULTI ×N (N combos).
  • Each combo, one at a time, like counting a hand aloud: its cards light up, its points float above them, and a line adds to the tally with the running total. "Fifteen +2 → 2, Fifteen +2 → 4, Pair +2 → 6..."
  • Bonuses, then edge power: each powered edge card lights blue and adds its level to the pips tally, not to steps. Pips buy jokers and special cards between rounds.
  • Power up: every hand, crib or starter card in the set gains a level (its chip ticks up, or appears at +1) for later scores.
  • The step total flies up to the step counter; the pips total is added beside it.
