How the shop plays

  • When: after every player's scoring grid, before pegging. You arrive holding your plays (every set you scored, plus the hand and crib counts) and your power jokers.
  • What's for sale: a fresh stock each hand for each player, from their own dice stream (shop, shop bot1). Bots shop by the same rules. Power jokers go in your tray for the rest of the game. Deck edits change the shared deck for the game: whoever is dealt the card gets it. Play edits change your plays for this hand only.
  • Tap an item to see what it does and what it costs, choose a target if it needs one, and choose how to pay. Undo takes back the last buy while you're still in the shop.
  • At most 3 buys a hand, shown as pips over the shelf. The extra-buy card (a placeholder) raises the limit by 1 for this hand and isn't a buy itself. The reroll isn't a buy either.
  • Leaving shows what everyone bought and what you take to the maze.

Stock, from what already exists

Decided on v1 (Bruce, 2026-11-17)

  • Pay with whole plays (A). B and C stay behind the toggle for comparison.
  • At most 3 buys a hand; a special joker or card can raise it (here, the extra-buy card).
  • The reroll is a card-sized slot on the shelf.
  • Deck edits are concrete offers from your kept hand. Play edits: dye, join and save all stay.
  • Each player has their own stock from their own dice stream; bots shop by the same rules.
