Crib Maze, joker mechanics, v3, 2026-11-14. Players feel the chance; they never see the tables. The match turn uses the same reveal (reveal.js) and tables (jokers.js). Every chance and timing is a starting guess.

What the player sees

A joker or extra card lands on the board and play pauses. Something random happens (a shuffle, a wheel or a die), the prize is revealed, and it flies to where it goes: the card's spot on the board, the stock, the tray or the pips counter. The rarer the prize, the bigger the reveal: a card flips quietly, a scoring card glints, an item or special joker bursts. The first time a player meets each kind, it's named with a one-line hint.
