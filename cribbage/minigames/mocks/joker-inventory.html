Joker inventory

Every joker, chip, badge, deck edit, extra card and joker-driven mechanic for Noddy Yookerspiel, built or not, in one place to ideate from. Ideas are dashed.

The game is about deck building. A joker that changes a card changes the deck for the rest of the game: made cards stay, painted and nudged cards stay changed, burned cards stay gone, and badges stay on their cards. The deck is shared, so anything you build can be dealt to a bot; an owner badge keeps a card's badges for you. Power jokers and chips are the player layer: they're yours, not the deck's.

The data is in joker-inventory.js: add an idea by adding an entry. Values and rules for built pieces live in config/ and rules.md; this page only lists them.
