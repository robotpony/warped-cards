**Crib Maze** is a free card game demo: cribbage where your points walk you through a maze instead of up a pegboard. This demo plays one part of it, the match turn.

- You get a 4 × 4 grid of cards, with your hand along the bottom, the crib down the side (when you deal) and the starter in the corner.
- Drag across 2 to 4 touching cards to score them like a cribbage hand: fifteens, pairs, runs, flushes and his nobs. The starter joins every set for free.
- Scored grid cards clear and new ones drop in. Hand, crib and starter cards stay and power up, multiplying the sets they're in.
- Use power jokers to rework the board (the swapper and the bomb) or pick bigger sets for a while (the reach), and race the clock. Your best set wins a prize.

Tap the HAND, CRIB, STARTER or POWER JOKERS tag, or the hand size counter, to learn more. Tap the top of the screen or the bar along the bottom for settings.
