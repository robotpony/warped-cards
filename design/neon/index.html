Neon asset tracker

Look C, "vector arcade", chosen by Bruce on 2026-11-14, for everything around the cards: table, board, tray, maze, HUDs, banners and effects. Card faces stay the original light cards (design/cards/cards.js); the neon faces were dropped the same day as too hard to read. Every visual asset the games draw, with its neon version and status. When a mock adds a piece, add a row here. Code: design/neon/ (neon-core.js, neon-cards.js, neon-maze.js); the app reads palette.json and shapes.json, written by node design/neon/export.js; checks run with node --test design/neon/.

Rules for every neon asset

  • Glowing lines and shapes on near-black. Every glow has a solid core, so it reads with the blur off, at 27 pt, and under Reduce Motion.
  • Colour is never the only cue: suits keep their shape, bots their silhouette, jokers their symbol, doors their number.
  • Lines and icons reach 3:1 on the screen. Anything drawn on a card face follows cards.js contrast (4.5:1 on the light face). Markers that sit over a card's edge carry a dark outline so they read on both.
  • Shapes are path data in a 20 × 20 box (shapes.json), never font glyphs, so the app draws the same thing.
  • Russo One for numbers and short labels only. Rules and UI text stay in Saira Semi Condensed.
