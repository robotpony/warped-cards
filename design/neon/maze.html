Maze tiles and pieces

Neon v1, 2026-11-14. Every tile and piece the maze draws, from neon-maze.js (the same calls as maze-art.js, plus defs()). Tune sizes here, then copy the values into neon-maze.js › S and run node design/neon/export.js.

  • Every cell reads as part of a grid: a faint tile and a dot at each corner.
  • Start and goal are icons: a play arrow and a diamond portal. Doors are laser barriers with their strength on a badge; walls glow cyan inside a magenta border.
  • Each glow has a solid core, so lines read with the blur off.
