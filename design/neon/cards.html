Contrast

Card faces follow cards.js (4.5:1 on the light face). Here: the neon colours used around the cards, against the table and against the light face they overlap. They all fail on the face, which gives the rule: neon never draws straight onto a card face. Markers sit over the card's edge as filled pills with dark text, or carry a dark outline (the star), and glows go around the card, not on it.
